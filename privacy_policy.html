Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

App Name: All Result BD

Developer: Abu Taher (Academic School)

Zero Data Collection Policy: We respect your privacy. All Result BD does NOT collect, store, record, transmit, or share any personal data, user credentials, exam roll numbers, registration numbers, or search history.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Abu Taher built the All Result BD app as a Free / Ad-Supported service. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use this application.

When you check exam results, apply for re-scrutiny, or use any feature within the app:

  • We do NOT collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do NOT record or log your exam roll numbers, registration numbers, or result marksheets.
  • We do NOT operate private databases or servers that store your search queries or result records.

All exam result requests are processed directly between your device and official government educational portals (such as the Bangladesh Education Board, National University, etc.) inside the embedded browser.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The app uses third-party services that may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information or advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) solely to display advertisements and deliver app functionality:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Google Ads)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

3. Device Permissions

The app requests minimal permissions required strictly for core functionality:

  • INTERNET ACCESS: Required to display official result websites, notices, and advertisements.
  • POST NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used to deliver push notifications regarding exam updates. No personal data is attached to notifications.
  • LOCAL STORAGE ACCESS: Used strictly on your local device if you explicitly choose to save a result screenshot or PDF to your device's Gallery or Downloads folder.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect zero personal data from any user, children's privacy is fully protected.

5. External Website Links

This app provides embedded browser access to official educational websites. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party external websites.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
